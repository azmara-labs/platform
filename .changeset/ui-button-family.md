"@azmr/ui": minor

Add the button family. `@azmr/ui/button` gains `IconButton` (a required `label` is its accessible name, type-enforced) and `ButtonGroup` (a named, joined group). New entries: `@azmr/ui/toggle` (`Toggle`, `ToggleGroup`: single or multiple selection, roving keyboard) and `@azmr/ui/split-button` (`SplitButton`: a primary action plus a menu of related ones).
