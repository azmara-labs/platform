Add `ScrollArea` (`@azmr/ui/scroll-area`), built on Base UI: a scrollable region with token-styled scrollbars that fade with the motion tokens (and stop animating under reduced motion). The viewport is a keyboard-focusable `role="region"`, so `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` is required by the types. `orientation` (vertical, horizontal, both), `scrollbar` (`hover` or `always`), `keepMounted`, `viewportClassName` and `viewportRef`. Adds the `primitives/scroll-area` L2 wrapper.
